How to play
  1. A S D F G H J K L are one continuous row: tones climb in a zig-zag from G and F outwards. space plays the ding.
  2. On each pad the amber name is the note, the small box is its key.
  3. Centre of a pad: full fundamental. Towards the edge: octave and twelfth. From the computer, the row below (Z X C V B N M , ., the dashed box) strikes the same tone on the edge.
  4. Shift + key for an accent. On touch, the more finger you press down, the harder the strike.
  5. The whole surface plays: just outside a pad you hit its edge; further away the note fades into a knock on the steel.
